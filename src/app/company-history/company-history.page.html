The JDM Experience Story

Connecting automotive enthusiasts to legendary Japanese car culture.

Our Origins

Founded by a collective of passionate car enthusiasts, JDM Experience was born out of a desire to share the authentic, thrilling world of Japanese domestic performance with fans across the globe. What started as late-night meets and expressway runs across Tokyo's famous C1 loop evolved into a full-scale guided tour service.

Expressway & Car Meet Culture

From the iconic parking area of Daikoku PA to the winding turns of Hakone's mountain touges, our roots lie deep within the golden era of 90s and 2000s Japanese sports cars. We preserve this rich automotive heritage by offering fans behind-the-wheel access to legendary vehicles like the Nissan GT-R, Mazda RX-7, Toyota Supra, and Honda NSX.

Our Mission

To deliver safe, unforgettable, and immersive driving experiences that put you directly in touch with real JDM culture, high-end performance machinery, and world-class driving routes.
